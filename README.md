# Dual Loan Strategy Planner

A lightweight web-based financial planning tool that helps visualize and optimize repayment strategies for multiple loans.

## Features

### Personal Loan Planning

* Outstanding balance tracking
* EMI-based amortization schedule
* One-time prepayment support
* Additional monthly payment simulation
* Loan closure date prediction
* Interest savings calculation

### Car Loan Planning

* Independent loan schedule
* Automatic surplus allocation after Personal Loan closure
* Dynamic payoff calculation
* Interest and tenure analysis

### Analytics & Visualization

* Detailed amortization schedules
* Side-by-side loan comparison
* Total interest paid vs. saved
* Debt-free date prediction
* Monthly cash-flow tracking
* Payment phase breakdown

## Key Calculations

### Personal Loan Strategy

1. Regular EMI payments
2. One-time lump-sum prepayment
3. Increased monthly payments after prepayment
4. Accelerated loan closure

### Car Loan Strategy

1. Standard EMI payments
2. Extra monthly payments after Personal Loan closes
3. Early loan payoff optimization

## Outputs

* Personal Loan Schedule
* Car Loan Schedule
* Loan Comparison View
* Interest Savings Analysis
* Combined Debt Summary
* Debt-Free Timeline

## Technology Stack

* HTML5
* CSS3
* Vanilla JavaScript
* No external dependencies

## Usage

1. Enter Personal Loan details.
2. Enter Car Loan details.
3. Configure one-time and monthly extra payments.
4. Click **Calculate**.
5. Review repayment schedules and savings analysis.

## Benefits

* Understand long-term loan impact.
* Compare repayment strategies.
* Identify interest savings opportunities.
* Plan debt-free milestones.
* Optimize cash-flow allocation.
